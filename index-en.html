01 / MY STORY

Try. Fail.
Get back up.

Before sharing my analysis, I was a photographer, an SEO trainer and a property investor. I learned by doing, with doubts and mistakes along the way. That experience is what I want to pass on.

01BEFORE 2020

I started
by trying.

As a photographer and SEO trainer, I first discovered investing through property. Stocks followed in 2017, then cryptocurrency in 2018. I kept moving without having every answer.

« Limited means. Plenty of doubts. And mistakes that cost me time and money. »

Property → Stocks → Crypto

02MARCH 2020

Then everything
stopped.

In January, my wife and I buy a house. The deposit takes all our savings. Lockdown arrives in March: Airbnb bookings and photography contracts are cancelled. The commitments remain.

« My back was really against the wall. »

A breaking point. A decision to make.

032020 / A NEW START

I changed
my approach.

The next morning, I do the maths. I go live on Facebook, film my SEO courses and offer them online. That same year, I start my YouTube channel to make finance more accessible.

« I put processes in place. »

Learn. Build a method. Share.
